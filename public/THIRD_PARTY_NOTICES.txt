Deployment notes
All model and WASM assets are served from this application's own deployment
by default. Their URLs respect Vite's BASE_URL. To explicitly use another
asset host at build time, set VITE_MEDIAPIPE_WASM_URL and/or
VITE_MEDIAPIPE_MODEL_URL. No remote fallback is used when local loading fails.
The controller also accepts explicit wasmUrl and modelUrl options.

Camera pixels are processed in the user's browser. This application does not
send camera frames to MediaPipe, Google, or another service. Model and runtime
asset downloads begin only after the user chooses to enable the camera.
